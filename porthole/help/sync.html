Syncing

Portage keeps a copy of every package available to you, the package tree, which has to be brought up to date (synced) to see new versions. Porthole does this for you.

On a schedule

Porthole's update service runs every hour and syncs when it is due: every seven days to begin with, counted from the last sync, including ones you ran yourself. After syncing it checks for updates, and the tray tells you if there are some. It syncs every repository whose auto-sync is on (emaint sync --auto). Change how often, whether to check after a sync, and whether to skip syncing on battery, in Settings, Updates.

Hover over the Updates button to see when the tree was last synced and what the last check found.

Syncing now

Press Sync now in Updates, or choose it in the tray icon's menu. It asks for the password (unless Porthole is unlocked), then syncs and checks in the background; the job window shows how it goes.

Gentoo's mirrors ask that you sync at most once a day; syncing more often can get you refused for a while. If the tree was synced within the last day, Porthole says so and asks first.

To sync just one repository, use Sync now in Settings, Repositories.

Check now is not a sync

Check now works out a fresh update plan from the package tree you already have, without syncing and without a password. Use it after changing settings, or to see the plan again after doing something by hand.
